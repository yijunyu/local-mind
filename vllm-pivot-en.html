Rust-generated kernels inside vLLM-Ascend

Inside vLLM-Ascend, individual AscendC kernels are swapped for kernels compiled from Rust by tile-rs, one op class at a time, each gated on bit-parity and a measured win.

A

vLLM-Ascend keeps the framework; tile-rs replaces kernels. The seam is proven — our kernels have served a live model path, and the override is additive by construction: anything it does not cover falls through to stock, at 0.89 µs per declined call. Speed is where it stands or falls. On RMSNorm every path measured slower than the operator it replaces, so that override now ships default-off rather than delegating at a cost. The operative override is the MoE gmm1 op, 1.17–1.19× faster than stock at the decode shape it serves — but that shape does not occur in real serving, so today it delegates every call.
